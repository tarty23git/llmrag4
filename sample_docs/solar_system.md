# The Solar System

The Solar System formed about 4.6 billion years ago from the gravitational collapse of a giant interstellar molecular cloud. The Sun holds about 99.86% of the system's total mass.

## Planets

Mercury is the smallest planet and the closest to the Sun. It has no moons and a day on Mercury (sunrise to sunrise) lasts about 176 Earth days.

Venus has a thick carbon dioxide atmosphere that traps heat, making it the hottest planet with surface temperatures around 465 °C.

Mars hosts Olympus Mons, the tallest volcano in the Solar System at roughly 22 km high. Mars has two small moons, Phobos and Deimos.

Jupiter is the largest planet. Its Great Red Spot is a storm larger than Earth that has persisted for at least 350 years.

Saturn's rings are made mostly of water ice particles. Saturn's moon Titan has a dense nitrogen atmosphere and lakes of liquid methane.
