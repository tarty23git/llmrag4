Acme Widgets Employee Handbook (sample)

Vacation policy
Full-time employees accrue 1.75 vacation days per month, for 21 days per year. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Vacation requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal.

Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone.

Expense reimbursement
Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days with an itemized receipt. Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to 60 USD per day. Expenses above 500 USD require director approval before purchase.

Equipment
New employees receive a laptop and may claim up to 400 USD once for home-office equipment such as a monitor or chair.
